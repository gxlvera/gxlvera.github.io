Motivation: Weight Sync in RL Could Be a Bottleneck

  • Traditional method: payload is the full model e.g. GLM-5: 744B params × 2 B (BF16) ≈ 1.5 TB per sync
  • On the critical path: Either the next rollout waits for the sync, or stale weights keep generating

Scenario 1

Training-Rollout in Same Cluster

effective bandwidth~25 GB/s
sync 1.5 TB≈ 1 min

~1 min vs. tens of minutes per RL step → not the bottleneck

Scenario 2

External Rollout

effective bandwidth~300 MB/s
sync 1.5 TB≈ 83 min

→ Rollout GPUs sit idle, or generation goes stale

Summary & Insights

Summary

  1. ProblemOff the RDMA fabric, weight synchronization becomes a bottleneck.
  2. ObservationIn BF16 or FP8, weight updates in RL are sparse.
  3. Naive implementationCPU RAM bottleneck, AllGather bottleneck.
  4. Optimization3.1× faster on 72B, lossless, works for both FSDP and Megatron.

Insight 1 · Why it matters

Cross-cluster rollout becomes practical

With weight sync no longer a bottleneck, trainer and rollout placement become independent choices: external rollout decouples training from inference.

Insight 2 · System design

Leverage the distributed setting of RL systems

Don't send all data to rank 0 or put all compute on rank 0: avoid the full gather, and reduce before any bytes move.
